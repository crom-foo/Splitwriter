Splitwriter v1.2.0 Portable — Windows x64

WHAT'S NEW
- Rebuilt TextBoard paragraph editing around consistent paragraph elements.
- Enter splits paragraphs; Backspace and Delete join adjacent paragraphs at
  their boundaries.
- Improved caret formatting for bold and italic, including selected text.
- Added paragraph alignment and paragraph preset shortcuts.
- Added 50-step per-board undo and redo history for the current session.
- Improved Typewriter scrolling and automatic caret visibility.
- Added Echo View start-point selection from the text board context menu.
- PDF export now opens the Windows print window without an in-app print preview.
- Paste is handled as plain text with paragraph breaks preserved.

NOTES
- Undo and redo history is session-only and is not restored after reopening a
  project or restarting the app.
- Bold and italic are inline formatting. Paragraph alignment remains a
  paragraph-level setting.
